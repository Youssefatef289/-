## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Minimalist Forms
**Learning:** In minimalist designs where visual labels are omitted to maintain a clean aesthetic, placeholders are often used. However, placeholders disappear as soon as a user starts typing, which can be disorienting for screen reader users or those with cognitive impairments.
**Action:** Always provide `aria-label` attributes that match the placeholder text for form fields without visible labels. This ensures the field's purpose remains accessible even after content is entered.
