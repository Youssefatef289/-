## 2025-05-15 - Improving Accessibility and Semantic Structure
**Learning:** In legacy static sites, semantic links (<a>) styled as buttons need explicit alignment (display: inline-flex, center) to match original <button> behaviors, and hidden labels (.sr-only) are essential for accessible forms without visual labels.
**Action:** Always verify centering and alignment when converting buttons to links, and ensure .sr-only is defined in the local CSS.
