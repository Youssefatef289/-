## 2025-05-14 - [Legacy RTL Hazards]
**Learning:** Applying `dir="rtl"` to a legacy project (Bootstrap 4.x/5.x mix) is extremely high-risk as it often breaks layout positioning, floats, and specific component offsets not designed for RTL.
**Action:** For legacy sites, prioritize localized accessibility improvements (lang tags, ARIA labels, focus states) over structural RTL shifts unless a full layout audit is performed.
