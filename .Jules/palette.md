## 2026-04-25 - [Legacy Form Accessibility]
**Learning:** In legacy landing pages where visual labels are omitted for "clean" aesthetics, using the `.sr-only` class (standard in Bootstrap 4/5) allows for full screen-reader support without triggering layout regressions or design pushback.
**Action:** Always check for `.sr-only` or `.visually-hidden` classes in legacy projects to bridge the gap between minimalist design and accessibility requirements.
