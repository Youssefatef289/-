## 2025-05-14 - [Arabic RTL and Accessibility Foundations]
**Learning:** For landing pages targeting Arabic-speaking users, setting `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"` is foundational for both SEO and screen reader usability. Additionally, legacy templates often use icon-only navigation (like scroll arrows) that lacks descriptive labels, creating barriers for keyboard and screen reader users.
**Action:** Always ensure the `<html>` tag reflects the primary content language and direction. Supplement icon-only interactive elements with `aria-label` attributes.
